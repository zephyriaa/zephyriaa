## About me

My strongest interests are **product thinking, engineering direction, systems design, and problem decomposition**. I enjoy starting with a problem, shaping the product idea, deciding how its major parts should work together, and communicating a clear implementation plan.

I am still developing my hands-on programming skills. The projects on this profile should be understood as **concepts, prototypes, architectural studies, and guided implementations** rather than evidence that I have mastered every technology listed in them. I am learning how to work effectively with developers, tools, and AI-assisted workflows while building a stronger foundation in computer science.

A useful way to describe my role is this: I am more interested in being the person who understands the product, defines the system, makes the important design decisions, and coordinates the work than in presenting myself as the person who personally writes every production component.

## How I approach products

I usually work from the outside in: first I clarify the user problem and the desired experience, then I map the workflows, define the system boundaries, identify risks, and decide what should be built first. I care about making complex ideas understandable through diagrams, requirements, prototypes, and clear documentation.

I am especially interested in the connection between **product requirements and engineering decisions**: how a feature becomes a workflow, how a workflow becomes a system, and how a system can be divided into understandable pieces that a development team can implement and improve.
